UK-PAKISTAN TECH COUNCIL
About us 
The UK Pakistan Tech Council brings together businesses, innovators, government partners, investors, and Tech leaders from the UK and Pakistan to support cross-border innovation, trade, and economic growth. Our mission is to shape strategic Tech cooperation that powers sustainable development, accelerates digital transformation, and fosters shared prosperity for both nations.
Our primary objective is to champion a positive and soft image of Pakistan while nurturing innovation, collaboration, and continuous education within the dynamic landscape of Tech.
Our dynamic platform empowers individuals to unlock their full potential, advance their careers, and make a lasting impact on the Council. We champion the highest standards of professionalism, integrity, and ethics, setting a benchmark for IT societies worldwide.

By uniting Pakistani IT professionals residing and working in United Kingdom, we establish a dynamic platform for networking, knowledge-sharing, and collective advancement.

Through our diverse community, we aim to catalyze positive transformations in IT, shaping its future, and delivering significant contributions to both the country we reside in and the country we proudly belong to.

Vision
A connected innovation ecosystem where UK and Pakistani Tech sectors collaborate to create world-leading digital solutions that improve lives, strengthen economies, and solve global challenges.
We aim to create a national network of Tech businessmen, Engineers and technical professionals of Pakistani Diaspora living in the UK, to provide a platform for networking, career advancement, Tech exchange and to develop the next generation of scientists, engineers and technical professionals in Pakistan. 
We are an inclusive support group to create an innovation bridge through links with Tech leaders, digital entrepreneurs, influential corporate executives, savvy investors, Angle/Seed Investors, accelerators, emerging start-ups in AI/Tech, professionals, advisors, policy makers & public bodies with aim to encourage investment collaboration on tech enabled social projects in between UK/Europe and Pakistan. 
The organization also plays the important role of a trusted facilitator in the growth of the strategic economic relationship, trade and investment flows between Pakistan and the United Kingdom. UPTECH acts as the intermediary for British Pakistani Nationals to understand how to approach the business community and set up business in Pakistan conversely for Pakistani Tech businesses that want to have a footprint in UK. It strives to do so through its vast network of businessmen, professionals, in both regions but also through strong relationships with governments and industry bodies of both countries.

Founder and Management Team

Roles & Responsibilities
High level roles and responsibilities will remain the same, however, there would be country/ area specific roles as well. Below Points elaborate the responsibilities of key roles in UPTECH:

Executive Committee
Provide strategic directions and oversee organizational matters globally and will have supreme authority to take organizational decisions

Chairman
Provide strategic direction and vision to the Council, guiding its activities towards fulfilling its mission and goals
Senior Vice Chairman
Act as a key leader and deputy to the chairman, supporting them in their duties, representing the organization, and taking on specific responsibilities, including chairing meetings and ensuring effective governance.
Vice Chairman
Assisting the chairman in providing strategic direction and vision to the Council involves guiding its activities toward fulfilling its mission and goals.
Chief Operating Officer (CEO)
Responsible for overall operations, implementing the goals, targets, and strategic objectives/direction, and company performance, motivates employees, and drives change within the organization and reporting to the board of directors.

President
Provide and execute the tactical and consolidated strategy as well as act as bridge for organization wide teams
Vice President
Direct and execute strategic and functional strategies domestically in order to achieve organizational goals
General Secretary
Manage all types of cross organizational communications, plan joint efforts and execute the joint initiatives along with other organizations
Joint Secretary
Manage all types of administrative matters for assigned i.e internal communications, arranging meetings, documentation and logistics etc.
Finance Secretary
Plan, manage, execute and record all types of organizational financial matters.
Operations Secretary
Responsible for executing the functional operations by leading the teams in various channels
Media Coordinator
Responsible for maintaining the social media channels and coordination with external media teams
Management Committee 
Committee composed of Vice President, General Secretary, Joint Secretary and Operational Executive mainly. Other members shall be brought on board based on mutual consent per requirement.
Meetings:
Regular Meetings
Regular meetings shall be held bi-weekly during the regular times of the year and will be documented for record purposes.
Special Meeting
Special meetings may be called by the Chairman or President with the approval of the Executive Committee.
Quorum 
A quorum shall consist of top officials and two-thirds (2/3) of the members.

UK-Pakistan Tech Council Code of Conduct
Code of Conduct
All members are required to give an undertaking to the effect that they would abide by the UPTECH Code of Conduct. The Code of Conduct will also specify the procedure for the action to be taken against concerned members for any breach of this Code. The following is the Code of Conduct prepared by the Executive Committee and adopted after approval by balloting by the Voting Members of UPTECH.
UK-Pakistan Tech Council Code of Conduct is issued under the authority of the Constitution of the UK-Pakistan Tech Council and is binding on all members of the Organization. ““UK-Pakistani Tech Council is hereby also mentioned as UPTECH
The Code of Conduct for UK-Pakistan Tech Council is a foundational document established under the authority of our organization’s Constitution. It outlines the principles and standards that all members are expected to uphold in their professional and personal conduct.
As a binding document, adherence to this Code is mandatory for all members, ensuring consistency, integrity, and professionalism in our collective endeavors. By adhering to these ethical and behavioral guidelines, we demonstrate our commitment to excellence, integrity, and mutual respect within our community and in our interactions with others. “UK-Pakistani Tech Council is hereby also mentioned as UPTECH″
The UPTECH Code of Conduct is compulsory for all members and adherence to its principles is obligatory. Therefore, it is imperative for every member of UPTECH to possess a comprehensive understanding and knowledge of its provisions.
Integrity
Members will consistently demonstrate honesty and ethical behavior in all actions and decisions.
Respect and Equality
Treat all members with dignity, equality, and cultural sensitivity, without discrimination based on any criteria.
Competence
Members will accurately represent their skills and experience and continuously strive to maintain professional competence.
Loyalty
Act with unwavering loyalty towards the Council and maintain confidentiality when entrusted with sensitive information.
Impartiality
Provide objective advice, disclosing any potential conflicts of interest promptly and transparently.

Ethical Conduct
Members will not pursue personal gain at the expense of the Council and actively work to enhance its reputation.
Non-discrimination
Refrain from engaging in discriminatory practices in any professional activities.
Continuous Development
Commit to lifelong learning, actively seeking opportunities to develop skills and knowledge, and encouraging fellow members to do the same.
Professionalism
Adhere to recognized professional standards, offering services and advice diligently and responsibly within their areas of expertise.
WHATSAPP GROUP RULES
No Personal Attacks:
Refrain from personal attacks in group chats to maintain a respectful environment; violation may result in temporary or permanent BAN.
Avoid Irrelevant Posts:
Steer clear of posting irrelevant content or discussions.
No Political or Religious Posts:
Do not share political, religious, sectarian, or biased content.
No Unauthorized Adverts:
Obtain prior approval from Management before sharing advertisements in the group.
Notify Admins of Number Changes:
Inform Group Admin or Management of any mobile number changes within 3 days to avoid temporary or permanent BAN.
Stay Relevant:
Keep discussions aligned with the group’s objectives to ensure focused communication.
Post Curfew:
Refrain from posting after 11:00 PM unless urgent matters arise.
Welcoming New Members:
Designate one member to welcome new members on behalf of the group to maintain efficiency.
Address Grievances Privately:
Contact Management directly for grievances instead of discussing them publicly.


DISCIPLINARY PROCEDURE
All members of the Council are expected to adhere to the Council’s Code of Conduct. In the event of a member wishing to file a complaint against other members for violating the Code, the following procedures apply:
All complaints must be submitted in writing and sent to the Management Committee of the Council. Any member is eligible to lodge a complaint
Upon receipt, the complaint will undergo investigation by a Committee vested with the authority to summon any member(s) deemed relevant to the matter.
Should the Committee determine potential misconduct, the implicated member(s) will be afforded a 7-day period to respond to the allegations.
Should the member(s) be found guilty, disciplinary actions may include caution, reprimand, suspension, or expulsion from Council membership.
If the complaint lacks merit following the Committee’s evaluation, the complainant will be duly informed.
In instances where the matter is referred to the Disciplinary Committee, formal proceedings will be arranged for a fair hearing of the charges.
If the Committee finds the response inadequate or no written representation is received, the matter will be escalated to the Disciplinary Committee.
For caution and reprimand, the Council will disseminate details of the circumstances and outcome to all members without disclosing the member’s identity. In cases of suspension or expulsion, the Council will, at its discretion, inform members confidentially.
Affected members retain the right to appeal the committee’s decision within 7 days, submitting a written appeal.
The Executive Committee will then review the appeal.
The verdict rendered by the Executive Committee on the appeal(s) shall be final and non-negotiable.
